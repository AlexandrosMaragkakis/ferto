          About this app & privacy
          
FERTO finds unfamiliar songs from artists in its owner's Spotify library. Recommendations are public. You can browse without connecting a Spotify account; signing in is only for the owner.

          
FERTO stores the owner's music catalogue, exclusions and sign-in session. Your browser remembers the theme and artwork colours, and uses a cookie to protect actions. Short-lived request counts help limit abuse; hosting logs may include request information.

          
Artwork and the embedded player load from Spotify. Spotify receives those requests and may use its own cookies. See Spotify's privacy policy.
